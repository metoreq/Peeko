Anima Lite

Each overlay has its own skin, display, position and size (up to 6; every overlay uses extra memory). Drop .gif, .webp, animated .png or .webm/.mp4 files into the skins folder, then press Rescan; transparent files give a see-through overlay. Turn Move mode on to drag an overlay, then off again so clicks reach your game. Ctrl+Alt+H hides or shows all overlays. Closing this window keeps the overlays running; reopen it from the tray icon.
